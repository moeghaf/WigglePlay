Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026. This covers the WigglePlay website at wiggleplay.org and the WigglePlay app, which shows the same pages.

In short: WigglePlay collects nothing about you or your child. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking, and camera video never leaves your device.

The camera

  • The games use your device's camera to notice movement, head turns and where a child is looking.
  • Every picture is analysed on your own device, inside the browser or app.
  • No video or picture is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, to us or to anyone else.
  • The camera is only on while a game page is open. You can refuse or withdraw camera permission at any time in your device settings, and the games can still be played by touch, keyboard or switch.

What we collect

Nothing. WigglePlay has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. The numbers shown at the end of a session, such as balloons popped, stay on the screen and are not stored or sent.

What stays on your device

So the games can open without an internet connection, your browser saves a copy of the game files on your device. This copy holds no information about you. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Spoken words

A few games say a word aloud, such as a number or the name of a shape, using your device's built-in voice. On some browsers that voice is provided online, in which case the word itself is sent to the voice provider to be spoken. Nothing about you or your child is included.

Who hosts the site

The site's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the internet address (IP address) of devices that request the pages and may keep it in its own logs for security. We cannot see those logs. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

Children

WigglePlay is made for disabled children to play with an adult present. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you contact us

If you email us or open an issue on GitHub, we see what you send and use it only to reply and to improve the games. Please do not send photos or videos of children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the project is open source, every past version stays visible in its public history.
